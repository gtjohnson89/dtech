Problem room

When night feels unsafe for both of you

A caregiver-safe way to name what’s happening — without blaming Mum, and without asking you to become a night nurse.

What we’ve heard

“She gets up to use the bathroom or check the door. I hear her, and then I can’t settle until I know she’s okay. Some nights I end up sleeping lightly on purpose.”

Common moments

  • The hallway check Soft footsteps after midnight; you freeze until they stop.
  • The bathroom trip that stretches Ten minutes pass. You wonder if she’s confused about where she is.
  • The front door worry Coat by the door. Keys in hand. You’re already out of bed.
  • The morning fog Neither of you slept well. The day starts frayed.

Needs & constraints

  • Alert the caregiver quietly — no sirens that startle Mum
  • Work when phones are charging downstairs
  • Respect dignity: no cameras in bedrooms or bathrooms
  • Setup under 15 minutes; no new daily routines if possible
  • Clear “all clear” so you can go back to sleep
