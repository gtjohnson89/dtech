How to choose

Same research spine. Three product faces.

All three keep Terra’s rule: the caregiver problem is the durable unit. They differ in who the first viewport serves.

Care Trials Primary user: caregiver. Bet: actionable home experiments that create product evidence. This Week at Home Primary user: caregiver community. Bet: weekly ritual + emotional recognition + visible progress. Care Build Foundry Primary user: George. Bet: operator foundry where Grok proposes, you approve, kits ship.
